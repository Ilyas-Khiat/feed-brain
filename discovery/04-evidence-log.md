| | |
|---|---|
| Trigger | Explaining the video to a colleague, mid-conversation |
| Wanted | Two advancements + their names; lab name; the video's big-picture framing |
| Attempt 1 | Guessed keywords in Google → recovered the *company* name |
| Attempt 2 | Scrolled YouTube history → found video → scrolled to the minute |

| Follow-on | Wanted to use the video as an entry point into deeper study of the topic |

**What Google solved:** the entity name.
**What Google could not solve:** *that video's* framing of which advancements mattered.
→ Corpus-specific value is real, but narrow. It lives in the framing, not the facts.

**Why history search failed:** YouTube's watch-history search covers titles and channels.
I remembered the content, not the title. That is the gap in one line.
