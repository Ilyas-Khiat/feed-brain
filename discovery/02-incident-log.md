# Test 2 — Retrieval incident log

Cost: passive, 3 weeks. Tests H3 (frequency), adds evidence to H2 (consequence).
Run in parallel with Test 1.

**Status:** protocol written, not run yet.

## What counts as an incident

A moment you *wanted* something from a video and couldn't get it:

- Couldn't name a concept mid-conversation
- Wanted to send someone "that video about X" and couldn't find it
- Needed a fact or source for something you were writing or building
- Knew you'd seen a counter-argument somewhere and couldn't locate it

**Does not count:** general wistfulness about forgetting things. An incident
needs a moment, a trigger, and a thing you wanted.

## The column that decides it

`Resolved by` is the most important column. If most incidents got resolved in
under a minute by Google or an LLM, then the archive has no job — that is the
real competitor, it is free, and it is already in your hand.

## Scoring after 3 weeks

- **Total incidents** — H3 false if < 8
- **Resolved faster by search/LLM** — if more than half, the archive loses on speed
- **Incidents arising while producing something** (writing, work, a decision)

That last number identifies your real segment. If it's near zero, you are the
casual-learner segment — the largest one, the cheapest to reach, and the one
without a budget.
