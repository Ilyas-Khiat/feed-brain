# Hypotheses & kill conditions

Written 2026-09-22, BEFORE any data collection. [What came of it](README.md).
Rule: moving a goalpost is allowed. Hiding the move is not.
Log every change at the bottom. Three moves on one claim = confirming, not testing.

---

## H1 — Decay is real

**CLAIM:** I lose the substance of videos I watch within 3-6 weeks, not just surface details.
**TEST:** Retroactive recall audit (01), n=10 videos from 3-6 weeks ago.
**FALSE IF:** Mean recall score >= 1.5 / 2.
**THEN:** If false, the premise is wrong. Stop. I remember more than I think.

## H2a — Retrieval friction is costly enough to matter  *(weakened 2026-09-22)*

**CLAIM:** Failed retrieval costs enough time/quality to be worth paying to avoid.
**NOTE:** My own incident (subject 1) was *resolved* in a few minutes by scrolling history.
Recovered, not lost. At ~2 incidents/month that is ~2h/year — not a business.
**FALSE IF:** Mean time-lost per incident < 5 min AND fewer than 8 incidents in 3 weeks.
**THEN:** If false, "save me the scroll" is not the product. Test H2b instead.

## H2b — Video-to-deep-study handoff is the real job  *(new 2026-09-22)*

**CLAIM:** The valuable job is not recall, it is using a watched video as a staging
point into deeper work — papers, code, a conversation. The video is an on-ramp.
**TEST:** In the incident log (02), tag each incident as RECALL or HANDOFF.
In interviews, ask what they did *next* after a useful video.
**FALSE IF:** Fewer than 1 in 3 incidents are HANDOFF rather than simple RECALL.
**THEN:** If false, drop this framing and stay with plain retrieval.
**WARNING:** H2a and H2b are different products. S0 passing on one says nothing
about the other (S0 is the problem stage, see the exit gate below). Do not let them blur.

## H2 — Forgetting has a consequence  *(the one most likely to kill this)*

**CLAIM:** When I lose a video's content, something concrete breaks that I can name.
**TEST:** Column 5 of the recall audit + the incident log (02).
**FALSE IF:** Fewer than 4 of 10 forgotten videos have a nameable consequence beyond "I'd have liked to still know it."
**THEN:** If false, this is an annoyance, not a pain. Either find the segment where it IS consequential (H4), or kill.

## H3 — Frequency sustains a habit

**CLAIM:** Retrieval incidents happen often enough that a tool would become habitual.
**TEST:** 3-week incident log (02).
**FALSE IF:** Fewer than 8 logged incidents in 3 weeks (~2.7/wk).
**THEN:** If false, no habit forms. A low-frequency need cannot carry a subscription. Reconsider as a free utility, or kill.

## H4 — A segment exists beyond me

**CLAIM:** A nameable segment has H1-H3 AND already pays in time or money to work around it.
**SEGMENT (pinned 2026-09-22):** R&D / technical people who use video as a *pre-paper primer* —
they watch to get oriented on a concept, then go deeper via papers, code, or colleagues.
Strong tell to screen for: they already maintain a notes system (Obsidian, Notion, Zotero)
that video content falls out of.
**TEST:** 8-15 interviews within that segment (03). Stop at saturation, not at a quota.
**FALSE IF:** Fewer than 3 of 10 show rung-3 evidence — an existing workaround they maintain, a tool they pay for, or a person whose job absorbs this.
**THEN:** If false, no budget exists in that segment. Change segment (once), then kill.

## Evidence ladder — what counts

| Rung | Signal | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Compliment, "interesting idea" | They are polite |
| 1 | "I'd definitely use that" | They understood the pitch |
| 2 | Problem story, unprompted | Problem is salient |
| 3 | Existing spend: workaround, tool, spreadsheet | Problem has a budget |
| 4 | Time committed: second meeting, homework done | It's a priority |
| 5 | Access granted: data, systems, their hours | Trust + urgency |
| 6 | Money committed: deposit, paid pilot, prepay | A buyer exists |
| 7 | Unprompted repeat usage, then renewal | The only real PMF evidence |

Rung 3 = floor for "problem is real". Rung 5 = floor for "build it". Rung 7 = only PMF.
**Rungs 0-1 never enter a decision, at any stage, in any volume.**

## S0 exit gate — all four must hold

- [ ] Problem stated in customers' own words, including words I wouldn't have chosen
- [ ] 5+ people in ONE segment describe it independently, unprompted
- [ ] 3+ show rung-3 evidence
- [ ] "What happens if they do nothing" answered with a consequence, not a shrug

Do not exit on: a big TAM, personal conviction, or interviews where I described the idea.
